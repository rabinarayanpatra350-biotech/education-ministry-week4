# Executive Summary — Week 4: Research & Policy Analysis

**Education Ministry Internship, Coimbatore** | Rabi Narayan Patra | 27 September 2026

## What the week did

Analysed the FLN policy arc end-to-end: policy landscape (NEP 2020 → NIPUN Bharat 2021 → Ennum Ezhuthum 2022 → NITI Aayog May 2026), effectiveness and citizen impact, three case studies, a formal policy brief with a scored options table, a government-database guide, and a presentation plan for senior officers.

## Key verified numbers

- Class 3 (govt) reading: **16.3% (2022) → 23.4% (2024)** — the highest ASER has ever recorded; subtraction 20.2% → 27.6%
- Equity: govt-school gains **+7.1 / +7.4 pp** vs private **+2.4 / +4.4 pp** (2022-2024)
- Delivery: 83.2% schools received FLN directives; 77.6% a trained teacher; **36.2% FLN funds**
- Tamil Nadu: Class 3 arithmetic **9.3% → 27.6%** (+16.5 pp); Class 5 govt reading 37% (below 46.3% in 2018)
- Decade view: Class 8 reading 74.7% (2014) → 71.1% (2024); only 29% of Grade 6 compare fractions (PARAKH/NAS)
- NITI Aayog (May 2026): 13 recommendations, 33 pathways, 125+ indicators; Rajya Sabha 363rd report proposes extending the FLN mission to 2032

## Recommendation

Extend the mission to 2032 while institutionalising level-based instruction, FLN beyond Grade 3, and competency-based assessment (Option 3, scored 18/20 in the report's options table).
