# Education Ministry Internship — Research & Policy Analysis

**Week 4 Task** | Education Ministry Internship, Coimbatore | Rabi Narayan Patra | 27 September 2026

A full policy analysis of India's foundational literacy and numeracy (FLN) arc, built entirely on verified official sources, with a scored policy brief as the centerpiece.

## The policy stack analysed

| Layer | Instrument | Key verified figure |
|-------|-----------|------------------------|
| Vision | NEP 2020, Sec. 2.2 | FLN declared the highest priority of school education |
| Mission | NIPUN Bharat (Jul 2021) | Grade 3 Lakshyas; universal FLN by 2026-27 |
| Financing | Samagra Shiksha PAB | ₹6,000+ crore to states in 3 years |
| Implementation | Tamil Nadu's Ennum Ezhuthum (Jun 2022) | ₹152 crore + ₹110 crore; 25.08 lakh students; 37,767 schools |

## Effectiveness verdict (three tests)

- **Did learning move?** Yes — Class 3 government-school reading **16.3% → 23.4%** (highest ever measured), subtraction **20.2% → 27.6%**; but Class 8 reading fell over the decade (74.7% → 71.1%)
- **Was it equitable?** Yes — government-school Class 3 gains of **+7.1 pp (reading) and +7.4 pp (arithmetic)** vs private schools' +2.4 and +4.4
- **Did it reach citizens?** Partially — 83.2% of schools received FLN directives, 77.6% a trained teacher, 74.5% TLM, but only **36.2% received FLN-specific funds**

## Case studies

- **Tamil Nadu** (design): Class 3 arithmetic **9.3% → 27.6%** in two years (+16.5 pp), the sharpest recovery in the country
- **Himachal Pradesh** (consistency): >15 pp arithmetic gain; tops Class 5 reading at 64.8%
- **Uttar Pradesh** (scale): Class 3 reading 12.3% → 27.9% (2018-2024)

## The policy brief

Three options compared through a scored table; **recommended: extend the mission to 2032 (as the Rajya Sabha Standing Committee's 363rd report proposes) combined with the NITI Aayog reforms** — foundational mastery over textbook completion, FLN beyond Grade 3, and level-based instruction institutionalised through state/district task forces.
